Live earthquakes

Every earthquake recorded worldwide in the past 24 hours, from the U.S. Geological Survey, checked for new ones every minute. New earthquakes flash as they arrive, and the view follows the current time, marked by the red line; drag to look back, and press ● Live to return. Magnitudes are often revised in the first minutes, and the timeline follows. Use the toolbar to switch between your local time and UTC, or click a region's tag to select all its earthquakes. Most small earthquakes are in the United States, where the monitoring network is densest.

Earthquake details come from the USGS feed in English. Other languages translate the timeline's interface (buttons, dates and durations), and Chinese also the magnitude categories.
